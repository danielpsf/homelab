Which icon should Tolon wear?

Tolon is an open-source desktop app that takes over your screen a minute before each meeting, so you join on time. It works only on your computer, needs no account and sends no telemetry.

We have six candidate icons. Pick the one you would want on your Dock or taskbar. You can also react to each icon and leave a comment. It takes about two minutes, and the order is random for every visitor.
