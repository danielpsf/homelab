A minute before your meeting, Tolon makes sure you notice.

Tolon is an open-source desktop app that takes over your screen a minute before each meeting, so you join on time. It reads your calendars on your own computer. There is no account and no telemetry.

We are choosing its icon. Six ideas are below, and we would like to know which one you would want on your Dock or taskbar.
