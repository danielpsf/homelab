See why the icons look like this

Tolon has one job: a minute before a meeting it covers your screen so you cannot miss it. The icons below are judged against that moment. The reminder and the calendars page on this page are the real Tolon interface, running in your browser with sample data. Nothing you do here is saved or sent.

  • It has to be friendly. Tolon covers the whole screen, so the icon sets the tone first: a warm object or a small character, not an alarm.
  • It has to hold up at 16 px. The menu bar shows the icon at 16 px. Every concept was drawn again by hand for that size.
  • It has to belong. The screens use paper, ink and one moss green, so the icons share that palette.
